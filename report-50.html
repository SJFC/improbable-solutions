Report No. 50 · The ministry of small injustices, with the department of villanelles

The ministry of small injustices uses woodpeckers and the villanelle to solve the problem of unanswered emails.

Problem (chosen)
Unanswered emails
Creature (dealt)
Woodpecker
Form (dealt)
Villanelle

An unanswered email is ghosted because it is said once, quietly, into a cloud, then overlooked. A woodpecker drums to be visible, on whatever resonates best, and in towns that is often the gutter, the chimney cowl or the satellite dish, a small insistence against the cold metal. No woodpecker has complained to the Ministry that they are waiting for a reply. Woodpeckers do not drum on clouds.

The department of villanelles suggests that every unanswered email is automatically repeated and resent at intervals in two alternating lines: the first line of the original request, and the last. This is to be repeated seventeen times, reminding the email recipient at dawn and dusk, at which point the recipient will find a reply in the original email. No new information is to be added.

Risks. Some recipients now reply only to the woodpecker, by rhythmic drumming. The woodpecker has not answered.
