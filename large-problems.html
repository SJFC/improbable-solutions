Policy · Large problems

Large problems

The Ministry addresses small problems only.

Large problems cannot safely be entrusted to poets. A poetic form will treat any problem with small measures of seriousness calculated in teaspoons. The Ministry recognises the risk of flippancy, and of offence, and rejects certain problems accordingly.

The Ministry does not recognise subjectivity. It has developed a comprehensive grading system for problems.

Problems found to be large

The following problems have been found to be large. Their listing is a record only. Where findings were issued in error, the matter has been referred to Ethics.

  • Report 51 · Rising sea levels · Woodpecker · Alliterative verse The Ministry expects fish.
  • Report 52 · Loneliness · House sparrow · Ballad There is room in the hedge for one more.
  • Report 55 · Food waste · Hermit crab · Somonka The Ministry has registered this as a marriage.

All representations will be considered. Some may be understood.
