Report No. 52 · The ministry of small injustices, with the department of poetic logic

The ministry of small injustices uses sparrows and the ballad to solve the problem of loneliness.

Problem (chosen)
Loneliness
Creature (dealt)
House sparrow
Form (dealt)
Ballad

Loneliness persists because it only considers one person at a time. A house sparrow bathes in the dust beside its neighbours, and at dusk gathers in a disputed hedge. No sparrow has applied to the Ministry for befriending services. Many residents have complained about sparrows.

The department of poetic logic suggests that each lonely person is allocated a ballad. They will sing the verses at dusk, in the street and the street will sing the refrain. Attendance at the refrain is compulsory. There is room in the hedge for one more.

Risks. Some streets now sing the refrain at breakfast, unprompted, and no longer wait for agreed verses, but sing their own variation, complaining of sparrows. The Ministry is treating this as a breach. The Minister for Loneliness has not been allocated a ballad or a street.

Final notice

The street sings songs of hedge and view,
the sparrows sing this too;
the Minister for Loneliness
sits quiet, out of view.
